# Community Code of Conduct

Tingjing welcomes respectful collaboration across languages, experience levels, identities, and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and constructive.
- Critique code and product decisions, not people.
- Respect privacy, account security, music rights, and third-party platform rules.
- Credit other people's work and preserve required license notices.
- Keep Issues and Pull Requests relevant and reproducible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, credential sharing, piracy requests, spam, manipulated Stars, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and participation that violates these expectations. Serious or repeated abuse may result in a ban. Security-sensitive reports should follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead of being posted publicly.
